About Neon Survivor

Neon Survivor is a browser arcade game about quick decisions, steady movement and chasing a new personal best. Pilot your ship, dodge the meteor field and collect glowing energy shards.

The game is built with HTML5 Canvas, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. It runs directly in a modern browser and does not require an account or installation.

Have feedback or found a bug? Use the contact method published by the site owner on the live website.
