How to play

Your ship is the bright blue pilot at the bottom of the screen. The field gets faster the longer you survive, so keep moving and watch the gaps.

01 / MOVEUse the left and right arrows or A and D keys.
02 / DODGEAvoid orange meteors. Each collision costs one hull point.
03 / COLLECTGrab cyan shards. Every shard adds 25 bonus points.

Survival notes

  • Look ahead, not just at your ship.
  • Use short movements when the screen gets crowded.
  • Your personal best is saved on this device.
