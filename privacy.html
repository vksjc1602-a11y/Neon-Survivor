Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: November 16, 2026

This page describes basic data handling for Neon Survivor. The site owner must review and update it to reflect any analytics, advertising, hosting or contact services enabled on the live website.

Gameplay and local storage

The game saves your best score in browser local storage on your device. The game code does not send this score to a game server.

Hosting and technical data

The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser type and request logs to deliver and secure the website. Refer to the provider's privacy information for details.

Advertising and analytics

This source version contains no advertising or analytics code. If these tools are added later, update this policy to identify providers, cookies or similar technologies used, and available user choices. Do not add ad code until the relevant account and site have been approved.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the accurate contact details published by the site owner on the live website.
